SnapCraft Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026 · Effective: July 18, 2026 · Scope: SnapCraft for macOS · Version: 0.1.0

TL;DR: SnapCraft does not collect, upload, or track any of your data. Your screenshots stay on your Mac. The AI assistant only makes outbound requests to the endpoint you configure, and only when you explicitly invoke it. We can't see, and don't want to know, what you screenshot.

1. Our Commitment

  • ❌ No screenshot upload: Images you capture never leave your machine.
  • ❌ No telemetry: No analytics SDKs, no "anonymous usage data", no event tracking.
  • ❌ No clipboard reading without consent: The app only accesses the clipboard when you explicitly click "Copy".
  • ❌ No persistent network: The app does not connect to any remote server unless you enable the AI assistant and explicitly send a request.
  • ✅ Zero networking by default: After first launch, SnapCraft runs entirely offline until you opt in.
  • ✅ All data is local: Screenshots, history, configuration, and AI presets live in ~/Library/Application Support/com.snap-craft.app/.

2.2 When you enable the AI Assistant

SnapCraft includes a built-in AI assistant that converts screenshots into documents (Markdown, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, HTML, PDF). It is off by default and stays dormant until you explicitly enable it.

To enable it, you must provide in Settings → AI Assistant:

  1. An API endpoint (e.g. https://api.openai.com/v1 or your self-hosted http://localhost:11434/v1)
  2. An API key

When you then click "Generate Document":

  • Sent: Your current screenshot (base64-encoded) + your prompt text
  • Destination: Only the API endpoint you configured (we do not proxy)
  • Storage: Governed by the privacy policy of the endpoint you chose (OpenAI, Anthropic, your self-host, etc.)
  • Can we see it? No. We do not proxy, log, or otherwise see your requests or keys.

Recommendations:

  • Sensitive contexts (legal, medical, financial): use a locally self-hosted model (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM) so data never leaves your machine.
  • General use: when using cloud APIs, you agree to the respective provider's privacy policy.

2.3 Error reports (opt-in only)

SnapCraft does not include automatic crash reporting. If you choose to report a problem:

  • You must explicitly click "Package Logs" in Settings
  • Contents: logs/dev.log (with screenshots filtered out)
  • You must manually choose to send it via email or attach to a GitHub issue
  • We do not proactively receive anything — this is an action you initiate.

2.4 Apple system-level data

macOS itself may record app usage statistics (controlled in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements). This is system-level behavior, independent of SnapCraft.

4.2 Retention

  • History images: until you delete them (per-item or "Clear All")
  • Configuration: until you uninstall the app
  • Diagnostic logs: 7 days (rolling)
  • On uninstall: macOS automatically removes ~/Library/Application Support/com.snap-craft.app/ but does not remove screenshots you exported to ~/Downloads/

4.3 Encryption

  • ✅ Full-disk encryption (FileVault): if enabled in macOS, all data is encrypted at rest automatically.
  • ❌ In-app encryption: not currently used (no secrets stored locally).

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

SnapCraft is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect any age-related information. If you believe your child has used SnapCraft without your consent, please contact us and we'll assist.

8. International Data Transfer

By default, no data crosses borders (because the app does not connect to any network by default).

Data only leaves your machine when you explicitly:

  • Enable the AI assistant and configure an overseas API (OpenAI in the US, Anthropic in the US)
  • Choose to report an issue and upload a log to GitHub Issues (GitHub in the US)

These actions are entirely initiated by you, and the destinations are ones you selected.

9. Security Measures

  • ✅ App Sandbox isolation (system-enforced)
  • ✅ Hardened Runtime (prevents dynamic code injection)
  • ✅ Notarization (verifies the binary hasn't been tampered with)
  • ✅ Private API detection (Apple automatically scans)
  • ❌ End-to-end encryption: N/A (no cloud data)
  • ❌ Two-factor authentication: N/A (no account system)
