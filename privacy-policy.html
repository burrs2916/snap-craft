AI-SnapScribe Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-25  |  Developer: BURRS

AI-SnapScribe (the "App") is a cross-platform screenshot, annotation, and AI-assisted analysis tool. We follow a local-first privacy principle. This policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Information We Collect

By default, the App does not collect or upload any personally identifiable information or screenshot content. All captures, annotations, and OCR results are processed locally on your device and are never sent to our servers.

2. AI Features

If you use the AI analysis / editing features, you must provide your own third-party model API key (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic). The related data is sent directly to the service provider you specify and is handled under that provider's own privacy policy. We do not store or relay your AI conversations or image content.

3. Subscription and Payment

The Pro subscription is processed through the Microsoft Store. Payment is handled by Microsoft; we cannot access your card or payment information. Subscription status is recorded only as the subscription validity period on your local device.

4. Diagnostic Logs

The App generates local diagnostic logs for troubleshooting, stored on your device. They are not uploaded by default and you may delete them at any time.

5. Data Security

Except for the network features you actively use (AI, Store subscription validation), the App does not communicate with external servers. Our local-first architecture minimizes the risk of data leakage.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
